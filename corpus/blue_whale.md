# Blue Whale

## Size
The blue whale is the largest animal known to have ever lived. It can grow about 30 metres long and weigh as much as 180 tonnes.

## Food
A blue whale has no teeth. It filters tiny shrimp-like animals called krill through bristly plates in its mouth. In the feeding season it can eat about 3 to 4 tonnes of krill in a single day.

## Breathing
Blue whales are mammals and must come up to breathe air through a blowhole on top of their head. Their spout can shoot as high as 9 metres.

## Babies
A newborn blue whale calf is about 7 metres long and drinks its mother's milk.

## Life
Blue whales can live for 80 to 90 years. They swim in oceans around the world.
