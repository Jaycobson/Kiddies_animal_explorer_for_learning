# Kangaroo

## What is a kangaroo
Kangaroos are marsupials, which means mothers carry their babies in a pouch. They live in Australia and eat grass and leaves. The red kangaroo is the biggest and can stand up to 1.8 metres tall.

## Baby joey
A baby kangaroo is called a joey. At birth a joey is only about as big as a jelly bean. It crawls by itself into its mother's pouch, where it drinks milk and grows.

## Time in the pouch
A joey stays inside the pouch for around eight months before it starts to peek out and hop about.

## Hopping
Kangaroos move by hopping on their strong back legs. They can hop at about 60 kilometres per hour and leap as far as 8 metres in one bound. Their thick tail helps them balance. Kangaroos cannot walk backwards.

## Groups
A group of kangaroos is called a mob.
