# Giraffe

## Height
The giraffe is the tallest land animal. A big male can grow up to about 5.5 metres tall, which is taller than a double-decker bus.

## The neck
A giraffe's neck is very long, yet it has only seven neck bones, the same number that people have. Each of those bones can be longer than 25 centimetres.

## Tongue and food
Giraffes eat leaves, especially from acacia trees. Their dark tongue is about 45 centimetres long and helps them pull leaves from thorny branches. A giraffe can eat about 30 kilograms of leaves in a day.

## Patterns
Every giraffe has its own pattern of patches. No two giraffes have exactly the same coat, a bit like a fingerprint.

## Babies
A baby giraffe is called a calf. A newborn calf is already about 1.8 metres tall and can stand and walk within about an hour.

## Sleep and groups
Giraffes sleep for only about 30 minutes to two hours in a whole day, often standing up. A group of giraffes is called a tower. Giraffes live in the grasslands and savannas of Africa.
