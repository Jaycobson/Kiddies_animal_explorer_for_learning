# Axolotl

## What it is
The axolotl is a salamander, which is a kind of amphibian, not a fish. Adults grow to about 25 centimetres long. It has a wide face that looks like it is smiling.

## Gills
Most amphibians lose their gills when they grow up, but the axolotl keeps its feathery gills for its whole life and lives underwater.

## Growing back
Axolotls can regrow lost legs and tails. They can even repair parts of their heart and spinal cord.

## Food
Axolotls eat worms, insects and small fish.

## Home
Wild axolotls come from only one place, the lakes and canals of Xochimilco near Mexico City. They are critically endangered in the wild.
