# Octopus

## Body
An octopus has eight arms covered with suckers. It has no bones at all, so its soft body can squeeze through any gap that is larger than its hard beak.

## Three hearts
An octopus has three hearts. Two hearts pump blood to the gills and one pumps blood to the rest of the body. Its blood is blue because it carries a copper-rich substance.

## Colour change
Octopuses can change colour in a moment. Special cells in their skin let them match rocks, sand and coral so predators cannot see them.

## Defence
When an octopus is scared it can squirt a cloud of dark ink into the water and swim away while the enemy is confused.

## Clever arms
Most of an octopus's nerve cells are in its arms, so each arm can feel and taste things on its own. Octopuses are very clever and can open jars and find their way through mazes.

## Life
Many octopus species live only one to five years. A mother octopus can lay thousands of eggs and guards them until they hatch. Octopuses live in oceans all around the world, from shallow reefs to the deep sea.
