Zebra notes (informal class notes, no metadata)

- Zebras are wild horses that live in Africa. Each zebra has its own pattern of black and white stripes, like a fingerprint.
- There are three kinds of zebra: plains zebra, mountain zebra and Grevy's zebra.
- Zebras eat grass. They live in groups called herds.
- A zebra foal can run within about an hour of being born, so it can keep up with the herd.
- Zebras can run as fast as 65 kilometres per hour to escape lions.
- Scientists have found evidence that the stripes may help keep biting flies away.
- Zebras often sleep standing up.
